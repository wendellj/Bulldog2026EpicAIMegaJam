# Press Release & Social Media: XR Lab Sponsors Student Game Jam Team

## Press Release (for BC Communications / XR Lab Blog)

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

### Bellevue College XR Lab Sponsors Student Team in Epic Games AI MegaJam

**Bellevue, WA** — The XR Lab at Bellevue College is sponsoring a student team competing in the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam, a global game development competition hosted by Epic Games from November 11-15.

The five-student team, drawn from BC's Digital Media Arts program and XR Lab community, will build a virtual reality experience for Meta Quest 3 using Unreal Engine and AI-assisted development tools. The XR Lab is providing development laptops, Quest 3 headsets, and workspace access for the duration of the jam.

"This is exactly the kind of hands-on, deadline-driven experience that prepares students for industry work," said Ron Austin, Associate Director of Immersive Learning at Bellevue College. "We're also documenting the process for potential use as open educational resources."

The Epic AI MegaJam offers $65,000 in prizes across multiple categories, including Best Student Project ($7,500 first place) and development continuation grants up to $50,000. Student teams compete against professionals worldwide, with entries judged on innovation, gameplay, visuals, audio, and creative use of the competition theme.

Faculty advisors supporting the project include Maria Sanchez Isaza (XR Lab / DMA), Renee Nejo (DMA Program Chair), and William Courtney (Game Development). Equipment checkout is coordinated through Larry Boykin at the XR Lab.

The team will livestream daily standups and document their development process on Discord and GitHub. Progress updates will be posted to Instagram (@bulldogsepicaimegajam) and LinkedIn throughout the competition.

**About the XR Lab**
The XR Lab at Bellevue College supports students and faculty in exploring virtual, augmented, and mixed reality technologies. Located in Library Building D, the lab provides access to VR headsets, development workstations, motion capture equipment, and expert guidance. The XR Lab is part of the Interactive eLearning Department and manages the NWXR EDU network for XR education across the Pacific Northwest.
