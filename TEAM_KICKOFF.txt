WHAT WE'RE BUILDING
-------------------

A Quest 3 VR experience using hand tracking. The core mechanic: you physically
walk around your room, and when you get close to your play space boundary, the
virtual world moves in that direction. Closer to edge = faster movement. It's
like controlling a giant virtual space with your body position.

We're still deciding what you DO in this space. Exploration? Puzzle solving?
Survival? That depends on the theme, which Epic reveals the day before the jam.

THE THEME

Every MegaJam has a theme that you interpret in your game. Past themes include
"Antiquated Future" (2023), "As Above, So Below" (2022), "Running Out of Space"
(2021), "It's Been a Long Time, But We're Not Done Yet" (2020), "Down to Earth"
(2019), and "Reality Is Often Inaccurate" (2018). Themes are open to
interpretation and can be taken literally or metaphorically. "Running Out of
Space" spawned games about storage limits, cramped rooms, and outer space.

The 2026 theme will be revealed November 11 at 11am PST during the kickoff
livestream. We'll watch together and brainstorm immediately after. Our edge-
proximity VR mechanic is flexible enough to fit almost any theme by changing
the context: a cave for exploration themes, zero-G for space themes, a memory
palace for nostalgia themes, limited visibility for darkness themes.

DAILY STANDUP - 11:00 AM EVERY DAY
----------------------------------

Starting now and running through Sunday, Nov 15th, we have a daily standup at
11am. This keeps everyone aligned and surfaces problems early.

If you've never done a standup before, here's how it works:

A standup is a quick team check-in. You answer three questions:

   1. What did I do since last standup?
   2. What am I working on next?
   3. Am I stuck on anything? (blockers)

That's it. Keep each answer to 1-2 sentences. The whole thing takes 10-15
minutes for the team. It's called a "standup" because you should be able to
do it standing up - if it's taking longer, we're doing it wrong.

Examples:

   "Yesterday I generated three background music tracks in Suno. Today I'm
   working on UI sound effects. No blockers."

   "I finished the main menu mockup. Today I'm starting on the pause screen.
   I'm blocked on not knowing what buttons we need - can someone clarify?"

   "I didn't get to work yesterday, midterm prep. Today I have 2 hours and
   I'll catch up on the crystal model. No blockers."

Being blocked is not a failure. Hiding that you're blocked IS a failure. If
you're stuck, say so. That's the whole point - we fix it together.

THE TEN MINUTE RULE

If you get stuck on something, follow this escalation:

   0-10 min:   Try to solve it yourself. Google it. Re-read the docs.
   10-20 min:  Ask the AI (Claude). Paste your error, explain what you tried.
   20-30 min:  AI can't figure it out? Post in Discord. Someone might know.
   30+ min:    No Discord answer? Tag me directly. I'll help you get unstuck.

Do not sit stuck for an hour in silence. That's an hour of project time wasted.
The ten minute rule keeps us moving.

Format: We'll do standups via Discord voice chat. Camera optional, but screen-
share when you have something to show. If you can't make 11am, post your update
in #standup before the meeting.
