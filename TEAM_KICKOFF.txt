WHAT WE'RE BUILDING
-------------------

A Quest 3 VR experience using hand tracking. The core mechanic: you physically
walk around your room, and when you get close to your play space boundary, the
virtual world moves in that direction. Closer to edge = faster movement. It's
like controlling a giant virtual space with your body position.

We're still deciding what you DO in this space. Exploration? Puzzle solving?
Survival? That's partly up to you.
