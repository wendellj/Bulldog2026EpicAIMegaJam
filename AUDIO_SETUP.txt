AUDIO TEAM GUIDE - BULLDOG MEGAJAM
===================================

You're making sounds and music for our VR game. This guide covers free AI tools
that don't require coding or technical skills.


WHAT WE NEED
------------

- Background music (1-3 tracks, loopable)
- Sound effects (footsteps, UI clicks, ambient cave/space sounds, etc.)
- Voice lines (optional - character dialogue or narrator)


TOOL 1: SUNO (AI Music)
-----------------------
Website: https://suno.com
Cost: Free tier = 50 credits/day (about 10 songs)

What it does: You describe the music you want in plain English, and it generates
full songs with instruments and optional vocals.

Setup:
1. Go to https://suno.com
2. Click "Sign Up" (use your Google account or email)
3. That's it - you're ready to create

How to use:
1. Click "Create"
2. In the text box, describe what you want. Examples:
   - "ambient electronic music, mysterious, slow, no vocals, loopable"
   - "tense orchestral soundtrack, building suspense, cinematic"
   - "relaxing synth music, retro game vibes, 80s, instrumental"
3. Click "Create"
4. Wait about 30 seconds
5. Listen to the results (it generates 2 versions)
6. Click the download button (arrow pointing down) to save as MP3

Tips:
- Add "instrumental" or "no vocals" if you don't want singing
- Add "loopable" for background music that repeats
- Generate several versions and pick the best one
- Keep descriptions simple and specific


TOOL 2: ELEVENLABS (Voice Lines)
---------------------------------
Website: https://elevenlabs.io
Cost: Free tier = 10,000 characters/month (about 10 minutes of audio)

What it does: Converts text to realistic speech. Good for narration, NPC
dialogue, or announcer voices.

Setup:
1. Go to https://elevenlabs.io
2. Click "Sign Up" (use Google or email)
3. Verify your email

How to use:
1. Click "Text to Speech" in the left menu
2. Pick a voice from the dropdown (try a few to find one you like)
3. Type or paste your script in the text box
4. Click "Generate"
5. Listen to the result
6. Click the download button to save as MP3

Tips:
- Write how you want it spoken: "Hello... is anyone there?" reads differently
  than "Hello! Is anyone there?"
- Use shorter sentences for better results
- The free tier is limited, so write out all your lines first, then generate


TOOL 3: ELEVENLABS SOUND EFFECTS
---------------------------------
Website: https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects
Cost: Free (included with ElevenLabs account)

What it does: Generates sound effects from text descriptions.

How to use:
1. Go to https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects
2. Log in with your ElevenLabs account
3. Type a description: "footsteps on metal grating" or "sci-fi door opening"
4. Click "Generate"
5. Download the result

Tips:
- Be specific: "heavy wooden door creaking open slowly" works better than "door"
- Generate multiple versions and pick the best


TOOL 4: FREESOUND (Free Sound Library)
--------------------------------------
Website: https://freesound.org
Cost: Free (requires free account)

What it does: Library of sounds uploaded by real people. Good for realistic
effects that AI struggles with.

Setup:
1. Go to https://freesound.org
2. Click "Register" and create an account
3. Verify your email

How to use:
1. Use the search bar to find sounds: "wind", "button click", "heartbeat"
2. Click on results to preview them
3. Check the license (look for "CC0" or "Creative Commons")
4. Click "Download"

Important: Write down which sounds you used and their licenses. We need this
for game credits.


TOOL 5: PIXABAY MUSIC & SOUNDS
-------------------------------
Website: https://pixabay.com/music/ and https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/
Cost: Free, no account required

What it does: Free music and sound effects library. Everything is royalty-free.

How to use:
1. Go to the website
2. Search or browse
3. Click download
4. No attribution required (but nice to give credit)


FILE FORMAT REQUIREMENTS
------------------------

Save all audio as:
- Format: WAV or MP3 (WAV preferred for sound effects, MP3 ok for music)
- Sample rate: 44100 Hz or 48000 Hz
- Bit depth: 16-bit or 24-bit

Suno and ElevenLabs export in correct formats automatically.


ORGANIZING YOUR FILES
---------------------

Create folders on your computer:

  Audio/
    Music/
      background_main.mp3
      background_tense.mp3
    SFX/
      footstep_01.wav
      footstep_02.wav
      ui_click.wav
    Voice/
      narrator_intro.mp3
      npc_greeting.mp3

Name files with lowercase and underscores, no spaces.
Use numbers for variations: footstep_01, footstep_02, etc.
