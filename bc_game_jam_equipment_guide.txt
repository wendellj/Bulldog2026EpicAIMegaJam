# Step-by-Step Guide: Borrowing a Dev Laptop & Meta Quest 3 for the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam

Use this checklist and template to secure a high-performance developer laptop and a Meta Quest 3 headset from Bellevue College. Because these items come from two different campus departments, you will need to follow two separate processes.

---

## Step 1: Claim Your High-Spec Laptop (First-Come, First-Served)

The Library Tech Hub does not do short-term two-week loans. Instead, they offer Quarterly Laptop Loans. You can keep the machine for the entire quarter, which completely covers your game jam.

* Location: Library Building D, Room D126 (Main Counter)
* Hours: 
  - Monday – Thursday: 7:45 AM – 8:00 PM
  - Friday: 7:45 AM – 5:00 PM
  - Saturday – Sunday: 11:00 AM – 5:00 PM
* Contact: library@bellevuecollege.edu or techhub@bellevuecollege.edu
* Action Plan: Go to the desk early next week (Monday, Nov 2nd or Tuesday, Nov 3rd). Walk up to the desk with your BC Student ID card. Crucial: Specifically ask for a "high-performance/developer laptop capable of running Unreal Engine 5." Do not accept a standard Chromebook or baseline notebook, as they cannot handle real-time 3D rendering or AI integrations.

---

## Step 2: Pitch Your Quest 3 Off-Campus Loan

Because Meta Quest 3 headsets are heavily reserved for on-campus classes, taking one home for two weeks requires administrative approval. You must submit a formal request via email immediately.

* Location: XR Lab – Library Building D, Room D126P
* In-Person Drop-In Hours: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday: 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM
* Contact: xrlab@bellevuecollege.edu

### Copy & Paste Email Request Template

Copy the text below, fill in your specific details, and send it to xrlab@bellevuecollege.edu. Make sure to CC your sponsoring instructor if you have one.

1. Your physical Bellevue College Student ID Card (required by both departments).
2. A sturdy backpack or padded bag to safely transport the hardware home.
