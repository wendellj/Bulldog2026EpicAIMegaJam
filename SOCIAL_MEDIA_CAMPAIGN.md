## Core Narrative: "Assembling the Crew"

Think Ocean's 11. Each pre-jam post introduces another piece of the heist:

1. **The Call** - Epic AI MegaJam ad crosses my feed. Game on.
2. **The Pitch** - I alert my people at the XR Lab. They're in.
3. **The Gear** - XR Lab sponsors us with Quest 3 headsets. We have hardware.
4. **The Specialists** - VR class friends sign on. We have developers.
5. **The Wild Cards** - Recruits from other classes. Artists. Designers. The full crew.
6. **The Heist Begins** - Nov 11. Theme drops. Clock starts.

This structure turns a team roster into a story people want to follow.
